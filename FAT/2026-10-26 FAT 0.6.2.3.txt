0. Crashes during test
   [ko->ok] 0.1 Crash on Weather Location Settings Dialog:
        - Add Weather Forecast docklet to dock and click "Location..." button.
        - Verify WeatherCityDialog opens centered cleanly without crash.
        BUG: On GNOME Wayland (Mutter), opening the Weather Location Settings
          dialog (WeatherCityDialog) crashed GNOME Shell with SIGSEGV in
          libmutter-18.so.0. The crash was traced to MutterBackend.vala invoking
          win.move_to_monitor(targetMon) during window placement, which
          dereferenced a NULL window->monitor pointer in
          meta_window_move_to_monitor before Mutter assigned it a monitor.
          Additionally, WeatherCityDialog set transient_for: parent and modal:
          true, causing Mutter constraint conflicts, and generic GTK popup
          windows were misclassified as the dock's hover window.
        SOLUTION:
          1) Excised all obsolete, unsafe win.move_to_monitor calls from
             MutterBackend.vala; Mutter Wayland natively assigns monitors via
             (x, y) coordinates in move_frame and move_resize_frame.
          2) Refactored WeatherCityDialog, WeatherWindow, and IconChooserDialog
             to pure independent toplevel dialogs: excised transient_for and
             modal grabs, standardized role to "help", and set explicit GTK
             center positioning, center gravity, and DIALOG type hints.
          3) Tightened _classifyWindow in Mutter bridge extension (v12) so
             internal GTK popups/menus are never misclassified as dock hover.
          4) GNOME Shell safety mode self-healing bootstrap automatically
             recovered the extension upon restart. Zero crashes on re-test.
   [ko->ok] 0.2 Elimination of Asynchronous Timers & Mutter Assertion Crash:
        - Rapidly clicking docklet items (Session, Weather, Preferences)
          to toggle them open and closed on GNOME Wayland (Mutter).
        - Verify zero crashes during rapid toggles or double-clicks.
        BUG: Rapidly double-clicking docklet icons triggered an assertion
          failure in libmutter: meta_window_unmanage: assertion failed:
          (g_list_find (workspace->windows, window) == NULL) followed by
          SIGABRT. This was caused by asynchronous timers (GLib.Timeout.add)
          firing 50ms after creation and calling show_all(), deiconify(), and
          present() on windows undergoing destruction (meta_window_unmanage).
        SOLUTION: Completely excised all asynchronous presentation timers.
          Replaced with deterministic, one-shot native GTK map signal
          listeners (window.map.connect) that activate the surface strictly
          when configured, disconnecting immediately and disposing cleanly
          on destroy without race conditions.
   [ko->ok] 0.3 GNOME Bridge Error-Latch Recovery, Dock Concealment & Throttle:
        - Verify extension error-latch recovery, unanchored floating window
          concealment, and fallback CPU throttling on GNOME Mutter.
        - Trigger interrupted extension deployment or parse failure in GNOME
          Shell; verify MutterBackend executes 'gnome-extensions reset' to
          clear the in-memory error latch and dynamically reloads the bridge
          without requiring desktop session logout.
        - Verify that while the bridge is unavailable or initializing,
          DockWindow concealment guard suppresses unpositioned window
          visibility at screen center, and Matcher throttles unaccelerated
          /proc polling to <= 0.1% CPU.
        BUG: Following an extension parse error or interrupted deployment,
          GNOME Shell entered an internal ExtensionState.ERROR latch.
          Subsequent 'gnome-extensions enable' calls failed silently. Without
          bridge negotiation, DockWindow mapped visibly in the exact center
          of the display, and Matcher fell back to a 1000ms /proc polling loop,
          consuming 6-10% CPU continuously.
        SOLUTION:
          1) Integrated 'gnome-extensions reset' in MutterBackend to clear
             GNOME Shell's in-memory error latch prior to reload.
          2) Added concealment guard in DockWindow.vala: on GNOME Wayland
             (!GtkLayerShell.is_supported()), window visibility is suppressed
             until backend positioning is confirmed, eliminating center-screen
             dock placement.
          3) Throttled Matcher fallback polling from 1s to 5s and cached
             known PIDs, reducing unaccelerated CPU usage to <= 0.1%.
          4) Restored tooltip classification in Mutter extension v13
             (lowerTitle.includes('hover') || role === 'tooltip').
