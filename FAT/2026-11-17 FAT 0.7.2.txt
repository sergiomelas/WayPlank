        ('wayplank -n dock-2'):
        - Launch a secondary named dock via CLI ('wayplank -n dock-2') with
          'ShowDockItem=true' and verify clean startup, D-Bus registration
          ('net.launchpad.plank.dock-2'), and 'SIGINT' teardown with zero heap
          or GVariant errors.
        - Bug: In AbstractMain.handle_local_options() (AbstractMain.vala:276),
          'options.lookup("name", "&s", out raw_dock_opt)' borrowed an unowned
          interior GVariantDict string pointer ('&s') into an owned Vala
          variable ('string? raw_dock_opt'), causing Vala's scope-exit cleanup
          to call g_free() on the interior GVariant buffer ('free(): invalid size'
          -> SIGABRT).
        - Remediation: Changed GVariantDict lookup format from '"&s"' to '"s"'
          in AbstractMain.vala:276 so g_variant_dict_lookup() returns an owned
          heap copy matching Vala's automatic scope-exit free.
   [ko->ok] 0.2 Crash (SIGSEGV at +5m 00s in SurfaceCache) & LauncherEntry int32
        Variant Assertion:
        - Run WayPlank across the 5-minute ('300s') SurfaceCache maintenance
          interval and emit KDE KJobTracker-style
          'com.canonical.Unity.LauncherEntry.Update'
          signals carrying 'int32' ('"i"') / 'uint32' ('"u"') '"count"' payloads;
          verify zero SIGSEGV at +5m 00s and zero GLib Variant assertion errors.
        - Bug: (1) In SurfaceCache.construct (SurfaceCache.vala:141), capturing
          'GLib.WeakRef weak_self' inside 'Gdk.threads_add_timeout(5 * 60 * 1000, ...)'
          passed an unowned 'GLib.SourceFunc' (no GDestroyNotify in gdk-3.0.vapi),
          causing Vala to free 'BlockData' at the end of 'construct {}' and
          dereference freed memory in 'g_weak_ref_get()' at +5m 00s (Use-After-Free
          -> SIGABRT/SIGSEGV); (2) LauncherEntryDbusService.unpack_payload() called
          'prop_value.get_int64()' unconditionally on non-int64 KDE KJobTracker
          '"count"' variants.
        - Remediation: Replaced 'Gdk.threads_add_timeout' in SurfaceCache.vala
          with a static 'schedule_clean_up_timer<T>()' helper calling owned
          'GLib.Timeout.add_seconds(5 * 60, ...)' (and moved WeakRef closures in
          Preferences, ApplicationDockItemProvider, and NotificationDbusService
          into static helpers with zero implicit strong 'self' capture), and added
          multi-type integer/double/boolean Variant guards in
          LauncherEntryDbusService.vala.
